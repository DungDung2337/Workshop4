## Executive Summary
The Q3 review covered performance across all business units. Revenue grew 18% quarter-over-quarter. Customer churn dropped from 4.2% to 2.8%. Engineering velocity increased by 23% following the adoption of trunk-based development.

Total new ARR for Q3 reached $2.4M against a target of $2.1M, exceeding the goal by 14%. The enterprise segment led growth with 6 new logos signed. SMB segment underperformed by 8% due to pipeline shortfalls in the APAC region.

## Product Roadmap Q4
Priority features for Q4: SSO for all tiers, bulk data export, and webhook integrations. Mike to finalize the Q4 roadmap document by July 19. Stakeholder review scheduled for July 23.

## Engineering Velocity
Story points delivered: 312 (target was 280). Major achievements include the microservices migration of the billing module, zero-downtime deployment pipeline, and automated database backup verification. Two production incidents occurred — both resolved within SLA.

## Incident Review
Incident 1 (July 3): API gateway timeout affecting 12% of users for 47 minutes. Root cause: misconfigured rate limiter after the infra upgrade. Fix deployed same day. Post-mortem completed.

## Incident Review (continued)
Incident 2 (July 11): Data pipeline delay causing stale dashboard metrics for 3 hours. Root cause: Kafka consumer lag spike due to a poorly optimized query. Fixed with query indexing. Monitoring alerts updated.

## DevOps and Infrastructure
Tom reported 99.94% uptime for Q3. Kubernetes cluster upgraded to v1.29. Cost optimization initiative saved $18,000/month by rightsizing EC2 instances. CI/CD pipeline average build time reduced from 14 minutes to 6 minutes.

## Security and Compliance
Annual SOC 2 Type II audit completed with zero critical findings. Penetration test scheduled for August 20. All engineers completed mandatory security awareness training. MFA enforcement rolled out to 100% of internal accounts.

## Data and Analytics
Nina presented the new customer health score model. Customers scoring below 40 are flagged for proactive CSM outreach. Early results show a 31% reduction in churn risk for flagged accounts that received outreach within 7 days.

## Customer Success
